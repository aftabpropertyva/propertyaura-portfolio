How to use UK property calculators

Use these free landlord calculators to test mortgage payments, buy-to-let cashflow, HMO yield, rent-to-rent returns, serviced accommodation occupancy, SDLT and property CGT assumptions. Start with evidence-based inputs, then run a downside scenario for higher costs, lower rent or a longer void.

For a deeper explanation of finance, Section 24, affordability and strategy selection, read the UK buy-to-let investment guide. These tools are educational estimates and do not replace advice from a regulated mortgage adviser, accountant or conveyancer.

UK property calculator FAQs

Which property calculator should I use?

Use the mortgage calculator for finance payments, the R2R analyzer for rental arbitrage, the HMO tool for room-by-room yield, the SA tool for occupancy scenarios, SDLT for purchase tax estimates and CGT for a first-pass gains estimate.

How accurate are the results?

The calculators are planning tools. Results depend on the assumptions entered and should be checked against current HMRC guidance, lender criteria, local licensing rules and advice from qualified professionals.
