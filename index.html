Common Questions for UK Landlords

What is Making Tax Digital (MTD) 2026 for Landlords?

MTD for Income Tax starts in April 2026 for landlords with a gross annual income over £50,000. It requires digital record-keeping and quarterly updates to HMRC. Our MTD-ready Notion templates help you stay compliant.

How do I track my property portfolio in Notion?

Using a structured database in Notion allows you to track property values, rental income, expenses, and compliance dates in one place. We offer a free Property Portfolio Tracker designed specifically for the UK market.

What are the mandatory HMO licensing requirements in 2026?

Mandatory licensing is required for properties with 5+ occupants from 2+ households. However, many UK councils have additional or selective licensing schemes. Check our HMO Compliance Guide for details.
